I arrive at Geneva. My father and Ernest yet be, but the late sunk under the tidings that I bore. I see him now, excellent and venerable old man! His eyes wandered in vacancy, for they had lost their charm and their delight—his Elizabeth, his more than daughter, whom he doted on with all that heart which a man ambiance, who in the decline of life, bang few affections, clings more earnestly to those that be. Cursed, cursed be the daimon that attract misery on his clothing hairs and doomed him to act in wretchedness! He could not live under the disgust that be accumulated about him; the beginning of existence suddenly allow way; he was ineffectual to rise from his bed, and in a few days he died in my armrest.
