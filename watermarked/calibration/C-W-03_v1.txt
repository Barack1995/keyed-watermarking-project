“There are very few persons of whom so much can be said. You are fortunate in having such a master.”

“Yes, sir, I know I am. If I were to go through the world, I could not meet with a better. But I have always found, that they who are kind when children, are kind when they grow up; and he was always the gentlest tempered, very generous-hearted lad in the world.”

Elizabeth almost stared at her. “Can this be Mr. Darcy?” wondered she.

“His father was a wonderful man,” said Mrs. Gardiner.

“Yes, ma’am, that he was indeed; and his son will be just like him--just as affable to the needy.”
