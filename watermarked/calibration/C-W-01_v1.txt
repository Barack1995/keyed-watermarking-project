“Nay, sir, you are my guest. It is late, and my servants are not available. Let me see to your comfort myself.” He insisted on carrying my traps through the passage, up a large winding staircase, and along another long passage, where our steps echoed loudly on the stone floor. At the end of this he threw open a solid door, and I rejoiced to see within a well-lit room in which a table was laid for supper, and on whose massive fireplace a great fire of logs, freshly replenished, roared and crackled.

After your travels, you will need to refresh yourself by using the toilet. I believe you will find everything you need. When you are ready, come into the other room, where you will find your dinner prepared.”
