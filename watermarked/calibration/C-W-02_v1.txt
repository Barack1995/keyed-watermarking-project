Mr. and Mrs. March exited the chamber with haste, and casting herself upon the bed, Jo cried and ranted tempestuously as she told the awful news to Beth and Amy. The little girls, though, considered it a very agreeable and exciting matter, and Jo got little comfort from them, so she went up to her refuge in the loft, and told her troubles to the rats.

Nobody knew what went on in the parlor that afternoon, but a great deal of talking was done, and quiet Mr. Brooke astonished his companions by the skill and passion with which he pressed his suit, told his ideas, and persuaded them to settle everything just as he desired it.
